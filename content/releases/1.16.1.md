## AS-IS

Everything from v1.0.0 to v1.16.0 is unchanged. **No screen, field, module or behaviour has been removed, renamed or moved.** This release fixes one bug that mattered and two that had not surfaced yet.

## Update / Modifications

### Fixed: Chats opened and then nothing on it worked

**This was mine, and it has been in the app since v1.13.0.** You were right to report it; it was not anything about your build or your account.

The chat list keeps a count for the badge on the app icon. That line of code was written **below** the point where the screen returns early while it is still loading. React identifies a hook by the order it is called in, so:

1. The screen opens, the conversations have not arrived, and it returns the spinner early. **Nine hooks ran.**
2. The conversations arrive, the screen renders further than before, and reaches the badge count. **Ten hooks ran.**
3. React counts hooks and refuses to continue when the number changes, rather than hand back the wrong state. It stops on the very render that was about to show the loaded list.

So the screen could be opened, and then it responded to nothing — tapping a conversation did exactly what you described. The badge line now sits above that early return, where it belongs.

I reproduced this outside the app before changing anything: the component's exact shape throws **"Rendered more hooks than during the previous render"** on the second render, and the same shape with the line moved renders normally. The fix is not a guess.

**Why nothing caught it:** the build is clean, the types are fine, and the very first render looks perfect. Only the second one fails.

### Fixed: a customer's own record came back empty from the people search

Introduced by me in v1.16.0's predecessor, v1.14.0. A customer's directory excludes customers — which includes the customer asking, so the endpoint reported them as not existing. Nothing reads that field today, which is exactly why it would have sat there until something did.

### Fixed: the drawer now dispatches what the library dispatches

My drawer navigated with `navigate(name)`. The library's own drawer closes the drawer explicitly when you tap the screen you are already on, and addresses the action at the drawer rather than letting it find its own way.

I tested both against the real router and they behave the same in every case I could construct — the drawer closes either way, and an open conversation is not disturbed either way. **This is not the bug above and I am not claiming it was.** Writing a near-equivalent of a library's own behaviour is a bet that nothing else depends on the difference, and there was no reason to take it.

## The check that should have caught this, and why it did not

This is the part worth your attention, because it is a failure of my own process rather than of the code.

**v1.15.0 added a check for exactly this mistake.** Checklist row 37: *"A hook added below an early return crashes only on the move from loading to signed-in, never on first paint."* I wrote it because a hook I was adding to `App.tsx` could have landed under one.

I pointed it at `App.tsx`.

The same fault was already sitting in `Chats.tsx`, where I had put it in v1.13.0, breaking the conversation list for everybody. The check that was supposed to be about this mistake said nothing, because it had been aimed at the place I happened to be working rather than at the mistake itself.

**A guard aimed at where a bug happened, rather than at what the mistake is, catches it exactly once.**

So the check now reads **every component in both apps** — 123 files, the mobile app and the platform. Row 37 is marked superseded by the new row 44. Running the new scan over the codebase found exactly one offender: the chat list.
