## AS-IS

Everything from v1.0.0 to v1.3.1 is unchanged. The company workspace and its tabs, per-company cron and retention, the shared-cache fix, client delete, Sales Order grouping, the Outstanding split and every earlier fix behave exactly as they did. Nothing was removed.

## New Features

### DPDP consent

- **The notice.** Each company has one consent notice, written here in Markdown or uploaded as a PDF or Word file. An upload's text is pulled out so the screen can show it as a page that scrolls — a browser's own PDF viewer cannot be watched, and reading before accepting is the point — while the original file is kept in the company's S3 folder and offered as a download beside it. Publishing creates a new version; earlier versions are kept, so an old acceptance always points at the exact text that was accepted.
- **One notice serves every company.** The platform keeps a default notice written with placeholders — `{{company.name}}`, `{{company.legalName}}`, `{{company.address}}`, the grievance officer and so on — which are filled in with each company's own details when it is shown. A company can use it as it is, edit it into its own, or upload its own, and go back to the platform's at any time.
- **The consent screen** at `<company address>/consent`: the notice, a progress bar, and an **Accept** button that stays off until the notice has been scrolled to the end and a sensible amount of time has passed. There is no Cancel — closing the window simply means nothing was accepted. The five purposes are listed separately: three are needed to answer an enquiry and keep lawful records, two (offers, analytics) are the person's to choose. Open it with `?from=app` or `?from=crm` to record where it came from, and `?next=/path` to send the person on once they accept.
- **Every acceptance is evidence.** Who accepted, when, which version, how long the notice was open, how far it was scrolled, when the end was reached, which purposes were ticked, from which page, with the IP and device. The same checks run again on the server, because a screen is only a screen.
- **Withdrawing** is recorded against the same row rather than deleting it — the Act requires the record itself to survive.
- **Cookie consent.** The website shows a banner with Strictly necessary (always on), Preferences, Analytics and Marketing. What each visitor chose is recorded and listed.
- **A new DPDP tab under Logs**, and a **DPDP** module under Settings, both permission-gated. Super Admin sees the same three tabs on a company's workspace, and the platform's default notice from its own side.
- **Reports.** Acceptance status and cookie consent both download as Excel, with the evidence columns an audit asks for.
- The website's enquiry form records its own consent row against the notice version that was live, with the lead it created.

### The company website

- **A small site for each company**, generic for any industry — nothing in it is written for one trade. Sections: Design & colours, Pages & menu, SEO & sharing, Announcement bar; Hero banner, Story, What we do, Featured items, Offers, Testimonials, Gallery, Closing call-to-action; About us, Enquiry form, FAQ, Contact hours & social, Downloads, Footer & legal, Policies & consent.
- **The builder** is a list of sections with a switch on each and an editor beside it, exactly as the sections are defined in the registry — adding a section later needs one entry and no new screen. Sections the site cannot do without are marked **always** and have no switch.
- **The enquiry form creates a lead** through the same path every other channel uses, so de-duplication, routing and the campaign tag all behave as usual. A hidden field catches bots, and the form is rate-limited per address.
- The site is published or taken down from the builder, and lives at `<company address>/site`.

## Updates / Modifications

- `Company` gained `dpdpOfficer`, `dpdpOfficerEmail` and `site`. Three collections are new: `DpdpDocument` (main database), `DpdpConsent` and `CookieConsent` (each company's own).
- The **Activity Logs** screen is now **Logs**, with an Activity tab and a DPDP tab. The Activity tab is exactly what was there before.
- The company's Settings menu gains **DPDP**. Like every new module it starts switched off in each plan: tick **DPDP** on the plans that should have it (Super Admin › Plans), then it appears for those companies.
- Retention Policy will keep DPDP consents for ever by default — they are the evidence of consent and are not touched by the housekeeping job.

## Fixes

- The website's announcement bar used the brand colour as a plain CSS variable; the theme stores colours as RGB triplets, so the bar came out invisible. It now reads the variable correctly.
