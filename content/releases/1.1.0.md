## AS-IS

Version 1.0.0 continues unchanged except where listed below. No module, screen, field or permission has been removed.

## New Features

- **Convert to Invoice** on Sales Order. An icon on each client row (and on each service inside it) opens an invoice pre-filled with the client, services and amount, where a **Discount** can be entered as a fixed amount or a percentage — each one calculates the other — with **Gross** shown as payment amount minus discount.
- **Company Name on a lead.** A new field on Add / Edit lead and on the client, shown in the Leads list, searchable, included in import and export, and filled automatically from channels that send a company, business or organisation name.
- **Integration › Needs attention.** Any delivery from any channel that could not be saved as a lead is listed with its reason, with **Retry** and **Retry all**. Each keeps its original payload, so nothing is lost.
- **Release notes** at `/release`, with the version shown in the menu.

## Updates / Modifications

- **Sales Order now shows one row per client** with its services, licences and total. The row expands to the individual services, each keeping Edit, Delete and Reminder exactly as before.
- **Outstanding counts only what is due up to today.** Dues dated in future months are shown separately as **Upcoming**, on the client drawer and in the Clients list.
- **WhatsApp Logs** rebuilt: a Refresh button, the payload opening under its row instead of a pop-up, an `OK 200` result badge and the provider's own reply in Detail. Filters, Resend, retry status and pagination are unchanged.
- **Sample .xlsx / .csv** rebuilt to match the Add lead form, including Company Name, Service / product and No of Licenses, with a "How to fill" sheet. Files made from the older template still import.
- **Compact, professional UI** across the company screens: tighter tables, cards, forms and dialogs. Every section and field is retained.
- **Client Journey** now has its own scroll area inside the drawer, so the last entry is never cut off.
- **Faster screens**: menu, profile, service and picker data are served from the local cache at once and refreshed in the background, and survive a reload. A save still clears the cache, so nothing shown is stale.

## Fixes

- Stuck channel deliveries now retry 8 times with a widening gap (about 15 hours) instead of 5, and anything still stuck is visible and retryable instead of silently dead-lettered.
- Database indexes added for the outstanding split, grouped sales orders and the failed-delivery list.
