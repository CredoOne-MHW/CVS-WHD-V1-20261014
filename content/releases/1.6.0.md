## AS-IS

Everything from v1.0.0 to v1.5.0 is unchanged. Leads, clients and the service board, client deletion by OTP with its archive, the Super Admin company workspace and all twelve of its tabs, DPDP consent, the website builder, the company apps and their GitHub build, per-company cron, retention, the shared cache and every earlier fix all behave exactly as they did. Nothing was removed or renamed.

## New Features

- **Chats** is a new module on the company side, at `/app/chats`, and a screen in each company's app. One-to-one and group conversations, with the message appearing for everyone the moment it is sent.
- **One-to-one.** Anyone in the company can be messaged by name. A pair of people can only ever have one conversation — the database enforces it — so a second "New chat" with the same person opens the one that already exists rather than splitting the history in two.
- **Groups.** Someone with *Chats › Groups* may create a group with a name, a line about what it is for, and the members. Members see it immediately, on the web and on their phone, and get a push. A group can be renamed, its membership changed, and a member may leave it; joining and leaving are written into the conversation itself so the history explains itself.
- **Nobody's contact details are shared.** A member is known to other members by **name and role only** — a phone number or an e-mail address never leaves the server in anything chat sends. That is written into the code (`publicMember` in `shared/chat.ts`), not a setting, so a group cannot be used to collect contact details.
- **Push notifications** to each company's own app, through Expo, which hands them to Google and Apple. A phone registers itself at sign-in and is forgotten at sign-out, so a shared phone stops receiving the previous person's messages. A token the device no longer holds is dropped automatically.
- **The scrolling notice.** *Chats › Notices* posts a line that scrolls across the top of every screen — on the web and in the app — in one of three tones, optionally also sent as a push, with an optional stop time. A person can hide a notice on their own device; the next one still shows. Tapping it on the phone stops the movement so a long notice can be read.
- **Messages are stored first and delivered second.** The reply to "send" is only returned once the message is saved, so a message is never lost because the real-time service, or a phone, could not be reached — at worst it arrives a few seconds later.
- **It works with no real-time service at all.** With none configured, every screen falls back to asking the platform every few seconds and nothing else changes; the header says which it is doing ("Messages arrive the moment they are sent" or "refresh every few seconds").
- **Unread counts, read receipts and "typing…"** on both the web and the app. A conversation never re-downloads itself: it asks only for what has arrived since the last message it holds.
- **Retention covers chat.** Two new rows on *Retention Policy*: **Chat messages**, which keep for ever unless a period is chosen — a conversation is a record, not a log — and **Dormant phones**, which are dropped after 180 days *measured from when the app was last opened*, not from when the phone was registered, so an active phone is never unregistered.
- Creating or changing a group and posting or stopping a notice are written to **Activity Logs** like every other action.

## Updates / Modifications

- **New module `chats`**, with sub-modules *Groups* and *Notices*. Like every new module it starts **switched off in every plan**: tick *Chats* on the plans that should have it, then give *Groups* and *Notices* to the roles that should administer them. Until it is ticked, nothing about chat appears for anyone — no menu entry, no app screen.
- **New collections in each company's database**: `chatthreads`, `chatmessages`, `pushdevices`, `announcements`. Nothing existing was altered.
- **`LogKindDef` gained an optional `dateField`** so a kind can age from a field other than when it was written. Only *Dormant phones* uses it; every existing kind keeps exactly the behaviour it had.
- **The app** (`mobile/`) gains the Chats list, a conversation screen, the notice banner and push registration, and three packages: `expo-notifications`, `expo-device` and `pusher-js`. The notification icon and tint follow the company's colour. **Existing installed apps keep working** — the chat screen only appears for a build that includes it, so rebuild each company's app from *Super Admin › Company › Mobile App* when you want chat on the phone.
- **New environment settings** (platform-wide, not per company):
  - `REALTIME_PROVIDER` — `pusher` (default when the Pusher keys are present) or `ably`.
  - `PUSHER_APP_ID`, `PUSHER_KEY`, `PUSHER_SECRET`, `PUSHER_CLUSTER` — the cluster defaults to `ap2` (Mumbai), the nearest one to India, which is what keeps a message under a few tens of milliseconds.
  - `ABLY_API_KEY` — only if you choose Ably instead.
  - Leave all of them empty and chat still works, by polling.
- Push needs no key on the platform: Expo's service holds the Google and Apple credentials, which are created with the app in *Mobile App*.
