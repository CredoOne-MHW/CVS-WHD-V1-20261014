## AS-IS

Everything from v1.0.0 to v1.13.0 is unchanged. **No screen, field, module or behaviour has been removed, renamed or moved.**

Two things are worth stating before anything else, because one of them was already true and the other was not.

**Cross-company search was never possible.** The part of the request about customers of *other* companies was already closed, and has been since the chat directory was written. The search resolves the company from the signed-in session and never from the request — there is no parameter to tamper with, and each company's records live in their own database besides. Nothing in this release was needed for that, and nothing in this release weakens it. A test now asserts it so it stays that way.

**Customers of the *same* company could be found, and that was real.** A customer searching for someone to talk to saw every other customer of that company by name and role, and could start a conversation with any of them. That is what this release closes.

## Update / Modifications

### Fixed: a customer's people search now shows only the people who work at the company

Staff, admins and the owner. Other customers are left out entirely, so one customer never learns that another exists.

Staff are unaffected and still see everybody, customers included — they are the ones who have to answer them.

A detail worth recording: the filter excludes rows *explicitly marked* as customers, rather than including rows marked as staff. Users created before the customer type existed (v1.9.0 and earlier) carry no marking at all, and under the other reading every one of them would have silently vanished from every search.

### Fixed: and the same refusal on every door that makes a conversation

This is the half that matters, and the reason the fix is four changes rather than one.

Filtering a list of names is a courtesy, not a control. The person you want to talk to is identified by an id **in the request**, so anything that only hid names could be walked straight past by sending one. There were three ways into a member list, and all three now refuse:

| | |
|---|---|
| Starting a direct conversation | A customer naming another customer is told that person is not in this company |
| Creating a group | A customer may add only people who work at the company |
| **Editing an existing group's members** | The same — this one is easy to miss, because it is a second, separate place that replaces the whole member list |

### Fixed: the client and lead search refuses a customer outright

Separate from chat, and defensive rather than a reported bug. The lookup that searches a company's clients and leads — its book of business — was gated on the **Users** permission. Role grids are configured by hand, and a company could tick Users on a customer-facing role without realising what else it opens. It now refuses a customer sign-in **before** the permission is consulted, so being right about that checkbox is no longer the only thing in the way.

## What is deliberately left alone, and needs your decision

**Conversations that already pair two customers are untouched.** If a customer started a chat with another customer before today, that conversation still exists and both can still see it.

I have not removed them, for the reason you have asked me to hold to: that would delete something without your consent, and it is not reversible. From here the options are yours —

- **Leave them.** Nothing new can be created; the existing ones simply remain.
- **Hide them from the customers involved**, leaving the messages in place and readable by staff.
- **Remove them.** Permanent.

Say which and it is a small change. Until then it is the first option, and that is the only part of this request not fully closed.

## Performance

No new requests and no new queries. The customer filter is one more condition on a search that already ran; the three refusals read a field that was already being fetched — each of those `select` statements gained the word `kind` and nothing else. Response times are unchanged.

## Logs

Unchanged. A refused attempt returns a 400 or 404 and is recorded by the existing request log like any other refusal.

## Verified

- `npm run preflight` — all five stages; **206 checks**, including **15 new ones**.
- The regression checklist gains entries **33** and **34**, both guarded by `test:tenancy`.

Three of the new checks deserve a mention, because they guard against the mistake rather than the symptom:

1. Each refusal is checked to read a field the query actually selects — **a guard on a field that was never selected is always `undefined`, and so never fires.** That is the quiet way this fix could have shipped looking complete and doing nothing.
2. The number of places `chat.ts` reads the user collection is asserted, with each one named and classified. A sixth cannot appear unnoticed; whoever adds it has to decide which kind it is.
3. The company id is asserted to come from the session and never from the request, so the part that was already safe stays safe.
